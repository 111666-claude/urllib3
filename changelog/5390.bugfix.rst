Fixed ``PoolManager.urlopen()`` not rewinding a seekable request body when a
redirect that keeps the body (307/308, or 301/302 for non-GET methods) was
followed across separate connection pools. The body's starting position is now
recorded before the first send and forwarded to every redirect hop, so an
already-consumed file-like body is replayed in full instead of as an empty
body. Bodies whose position cannot be recorded (for example ``tell()`` raises)
keep their previous behavior and are not affected on a 303, which drops the
body.
